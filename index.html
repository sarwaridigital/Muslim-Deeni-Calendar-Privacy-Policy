1 Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Muslim Deeni Calendar ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal data is handled responsibly, transparently, and securely.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Muslim Deeni Calendar mobile application distributed via the Google Play Store. It outlines the specific types of information we access or collect, how that information is used, how it is safeguarded on your Android device, and your rights as a user.

Core Commitment: Muslim Deeni Calendar is built as a Sadqah-e-Jariyah (noble ongoing Islamic benefit). It does not track your browsing habits, does not build user profiles, does not serve behavioral advertisements, and does not transmit your private worship data to external servers.

2 Information We Access and Collect

To provide core Islamic features (such as calculating the exact astronomical prayer times, pointing towards the Kaaba, and sounding Azaan alarms), our application requires access to specific device capabilities:

A. Geographic Location Data (Precise & Approximate)

  • What is accessed: Your device's latitude, longitude, and approximate city name via GPS or network location providers.
  • Why it is needed:
    1. Accurate Prayer Times: Calculating astronomical solar positions (Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) according to your chosen Islamic calculation method (e.g., University of Islamic Sciences Karachi, Umm Al-Qura, ISNA, MWL) and school of jurisprudence (Hanafi/Shafi'i).
    2. Qibla Compass: Computing the true geodesic heading from your current coordinate to the Holy Kaaba in Makkah (21.4225° N, 39.8262° E).
    3. Nearby Mosques: Allowing you to locate masjids in your vicinity via external map intent.
  • How it is handled: Your location is processed strictly on your device. It is saved in the local Android SQLite database (`AppDatabase`) on your phone and is never uploaded or sold to any cloud servers or third parties.
  • Manual Fallback: If you do not wish to grant GPS permissions, the app provides a complete offline city selection tool allowing you to select your city manually without giving any location permission.

B. Personal Notes, Bookmarks & Worship Progress

  • What is collected: Personal notes attached to Hijri calendar dates, Ayah bookmarks in the Holy Quran, Qaza Namaz counts, and daily Tasbeeh counts.
  • Where it is stored: Stored 100% locally in your phone's internal storage sandbox using Android Room Database. No account creation is mandatory, and your private worship records remain solely on your device.

C. Audio Streaming and Media Cache

  • What is accessed: Holy Quran audio recitations and Azaan audio files.
  • How it is handled: Audio files are fetched directly over secure HTTPS from public, community-recognized Islamic CDNs (such as EveryAyah and Quran.com API). No user personal identifiers are sent along with these network requests.

4 Third-Party Services & Integrations

We do not integrate with third-party tracking services or data brokers. The app interacts with trusted service providers only as follows:

  • Google Play Services (Fused Location Provider): Used exclusively on-device to retrieve system GPS coordinates when the user requests auto-detection. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Public Islamic Content CDNs: High-quality Quran recitations and tafseer texts are retrieved directly from verified open Islamic repositories via encrypted HTTPS protocols.
  • External Map Applications: When you click "Find Nearby Mosques", the app launches your installed mapping application (such as Google Maps) via standard Android intents with a search query. No personal information is transmitted by our app.

5 Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Muslim Deeni Calendar is designed for the entire family, including young learners seeking to memorize Islamic kalimas, Quranic surahs, and daily supplications.

Protection of Minors: We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in the European Union). Since the application does not require user registration and processes all essential calculations on-device, parents and educators can be completely assured that children's privacy is fully preserved.

6 Data Retention, Control & Deletion

Because your data is stored locally in your device's isolated app storage:

  • You have total ownership: You can edit, modify, or erase your notes, bookmarks, and settings at any time directly through the app UI.
  • Complete deletion: Clearing the application's data via your Android device's Settings > Apps > Muslim Deeni Calendar > Storage > Clear Storage or uninstalling the app permanently and irreversibly deletes all stored preferences, notes, and local database entries from your device.

7 Security of Your Information

We implement rigorous technical safeguards to protect your data:

  • Android App Sandboxing: All local database entries (notes, city coordinates, preferences) are stored in your private internal application directory which cannot be read by other installed applications.
  • Encrypted Connections: Any web request (e.g. streaming audio or downloading recitation files) is strictly enforced over TLS/HTTPS encryption.
